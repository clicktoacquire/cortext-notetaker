Support & documentation

Cortext Notetaker · Click To Acquire LLC · Effective November 17, 2026

Contact support

Email robert@clicktoacquire.com for setup, meeting capture, removal and privacy requests. Support hours are Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM US Eastern. You will receive a first reply within 2 business days.

Include a short description of the issue, the approximate meeting date and time with timezone, and any non-sensitive error message. Do not send passwords, authorization codes, tokens, full callback URLs, meeting passcodes or full private transcripts by email.

Documentation

Cortext Notetaker is an operator-assisted Zoom meeting bot. The local service runs on the operator’s computer. It saves available Zoom closed captions with speaker names and meeting chat the bot can see. Audio and video recording are off by default.

It is for business users aged 18 and older in the United States and supports English. It is not offered in the European Union.

Arrange access and authorize Zoom

  1. Email robert@clicktoacquire.com to arrange access and a setup session.
  2. The operator provides the authorization route and guides the local connection setup. Sign in to your own Zoom account and review the requested permissions: read your Zoom user profile, obtain an on-behalf-of token for the meeting connection, view your scheduled meetings (so the bot can join the ones you choose) and, only if you turn it on, obtain a local recording token for meetings you host (for more accurate transcripts).
  3. Complete the connection setup with the operator. Authorization credentials must be handled through the arranged secure setup process, not ordinary email or chat.
  4. Agree on the meeting to join, participant notices and any required consent. The operator launches the bot for that meeting while the connected Zoom user participates. The host must admit the bot if admission is required.

Capture captions and chat

The bot appears as “Cortext notetaker.” Make Zoom captions available and follow the meeting’s content-access permissions. Caption quality and speaker attribution depend on Zoom. Ask the operator for help reviewing the saved transcript or notes.

The operator may use Anthropic’s Claude to summarize saved captions into meeting notes stored on the operator’s computer. Review generated notes for accuracy.

Private questions and answers

Private in-meeting questions and answers are not available in this version because the app cannot yet verify that the asking participant is the connected Zoom account owner. Sending a question or changing your display name will not enable this feature. This version does not use Google Calendar or BigQuery as Q&A context.

Stop use or remove the app

  1. Ask the meeting host or operator to remove the bot from an ongoing meeting when you want capture to stop.
  2. To remove authorization, sign in to Zoom App Marketplace, choose Manage → Added Apps, select Cortext Notetaker and choose Remove.
  3. App removal does not automatically delete saved captions, chat, notes or logs. Email robert@clicktoacquire.com for access, correction or deletion. We verify your identity and authority, then delete the data we hold covered by your verified request within 30 days of verification.
  4. To reconnect, contact support and authorize the app again with your own Zoom account.

Troubleshooting

  • The bot does not join: check with the operator that the local service is available, the intended Zoom user is connected and participating, the meeting link is correct, and the host admits the bot. Zoom account restrictions may affect joining.
  • Captions are missing or inaccurate: check caption availability and permissions with the host. The app can save only the captions it receives.
  • The bot leaves early: the current version is configured for a maximum session of 15 minutes and five-minute waiting-for-host, waiting-room and connected-user-absence timeouts. Contact the operator to investigate an unexpected departure.
  • No chat answer arrives: private in-meeting Q&A is unavailable in this version. Contact support for help with saved captions and notes.
  • A note is wrong: compare it with the meeting and saved captions; contact support to request correction of information we hold.

Data and privacy requests

Contact robert@clicktoacquire.com. Give enough information to identify the meeting and your relationship to it without including private meeting content. See the Privacy policy for retention and request details and the Terms of Use for service responsibilities.
