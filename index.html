Cortext Notetaker

Cortext Notetaker · Click To Acquire LLC · Effective November 17, 2026

Cortext Notetaker helps business users keep a written record of authorized Zoom meetings. It saves available Zoom closed captions with speaker names and meeting chat the bot can see. Audio and video recording are off by default.

The operator may use Anthropic’s Claude to summarize saved captions into meeting notes stored on the operator’s computer.

Private in-meeting questions and answers are not available in this version because the app cannot yet verify that the asking participant is the connected Zoom account owner.

Access and support

Access is arranged with the operator. The app is for business users aged 18 and older in the United States and supports English. It is not offered in the European Union.

Contact robert@clicktoacquire.com to arrange access or get help. Support hours are Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM US Eastern. You will receive a first reply within 2 business days.
