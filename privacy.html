Privacy policy

Cortext Notetaker · Click To Acquire LLC · Effective November 17, 2026

Who we are

Click To Acquire LLC operates Cortext Notetaker. Our address is 2598 E. Sunrise Blvd, Suite 2104, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33304, USA. Contact robert@clicktoacquire.com for privacy questions and requests.

Information we collect

  • Zoom connection information: profile information returned when you authorize the app, your Zoom user identifier, authorization credentials and connection status.
  • Meeting information: meeting links and metadata, participant names and identifiers, available Zoom closed captions with speaker names, and meeting chat the bot can see.
  • Notes and operational records: meeting notes prepared from saved captions, local meeting event logs, service status and error records, and diagnostic artifacts when generated.
  • Scheduled meetings: the topic, time and join link of meetings on your Zoom account, used only to join the meetings you choose automatically.
  • Meeting audio (only if you turn it on): in meetings you host, with Zoom's local recording permission, the app can receive meeting audio to produce more accurate transcripts. This audio is stored on the operator's computer and is covered by the same retention and deletion terms as captions.
  • Support information: information you provide when asking for help or making a privacy request.

Audio and video recording are off by default. Caption accuracy and availability depend on Zoom and meeting settings.

How we use information

We use this information to connect to authorized Zoom meetings, save captions and accessible chat, prepare meeting notes, provide support, address privacy requests and troubleshoot operation. The operator may use Anthropic’s Claude to summarize saved captions into meeting notes stored on the operator’s computer.

Private in-meeting questions and answers are not available in this version because the app cannot yet verify that the asking participant is the connected Zoom account owner. This version does not use Google Calendar or BigQuery as Q&A context.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use meeting content to train AI models.

Services that receive information

  • Zoom: provides meetings, sign-in and app authorization and processes the meeting and connection information needed for those services.
  • Anthropic: receives saved caption content when the operator uses Claude to prepare meeting notes or summaries. This processing takes place outside the operator’s computer.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts these public pages and receives ordinary website request information, such as an IP address and browser request details, when you visit them. Meeting transcripts are not published on these pages.

Google Calendar and BigQuery are not used for Q&A context in this version. Each external service handles information it receives under its own applicable terms and policies. Our statement about our own use of meeting content for AI training does not describe vendors’ independent practices.

Storage and retention

Application data, including saved captions, accessible chat, meeting notes and local operational records, is stored on the operator’s computer in the United States. Use of Zoom and Claude involves external processing; local application storage does not mean all processing stays on that computer.

Meeting event log entries remain in the active log for 30 days from the time each entry is logged, then move to a private local archive. Archive maintenance runs when the service starts and hourly while it is running; after downtime it resumes at startup. Archives do not have an automatic expiry.

The 30-day active-log period does not automatically delete saved transcripts, chat, notes, connection records, diagnostic artifacts or archived entries. Those records may remain until deletion is requested. Information held independently by external services is subject to those services’ retention practices.

Your requests

Email robert@clicktoacquire.com to request access, correction or deletion of your information. Provide enough information to identify the meeting and your relationship to it, without sending a full transcript, passwords or authorization credentials. We verify your identity and authority before acting on a request.

We delete the data we hold covered by a verified request within 30 days of verification. This includes relevant local copies and private archives. We explain any limits relating to information held independently by external services.

Where applicable law gives you additional rights, you may also request restriction or portability, object to processing, withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, or complain to the relevant privacy authority.

Meeting permissions and removal

Users must have authority to use the app and provide required participant notices and obtain any required consent. Tell the meeting host or operator if you object to the bot’s presence or want it removed.

You can remove the app in Zoom App Marketplace under Manage → Added Apps → Cortext Notetaker → Remove. Removing authorization is separate from removing the bot from an ongoing meeting and deleting existing data. Contact the operator for those requests; see Support & documentation.

Audience and policy changes

The app is for business users aged 18 and older in the United States, supports English and is not offered in the European Union. It is not intended for children.

We will publish policy changes on this page and update the effective date. For questions, contact robert@clicktoacquire.com.
