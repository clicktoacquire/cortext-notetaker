What the service does

Cortext Notetaker joins authorized Zoom meetings as “Cortext notetaker” and saves available Zoom closed captions with speaker names and meeting chat the bot can see. Audio and video recording are off by default. The operator may use Anthropic’s Claude to summarize saved captions into meeting notes stored on the operator’s computer.

Private in-meeting questions and answers are not available in this version because the app cannot yet verify that the asking participant is the connected Zoom account owner. This version does not use Google Calendar or BigQuery as Q&A context.

Availability depends on the operator’s computer, network, Zoom, meeting settings and any external service used. The current version is configured for a maximum bot session of 15 minutes. We do not promise uninterrupted availability or complete capture of every meeting.
